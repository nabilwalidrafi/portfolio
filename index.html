Research Projects

🧠
MedGemma vs GPT-4 — Zero-Shot Medical Disease Classification
Benchmarked open-source MedGemma-4b-it against proprietary GPT-4 on zero-shot classification across 6 disease categories using medical images. MedGemma achieved 80.37% vs GPT-4's 69.58% — demonstrating that a fine-tunable open-source foundational model outperforms a closed proprietary system on medical imaging tasks, with significant implications for resource-constrained clinical deployment.

🦟
Dengue Classification — Stacked MLP Ensemble with XAI
Proposed a stacked MLP meta-learner combining CatBoost raw prediction scores and TabNet probability estimates on 1,523 hematological samples (19 features: platelet count, WBC, RDW-CV, hemoglobin, MPV, etc.). Three-layer MLP (256→128→64, ReLU, dropout 0.4/0.3, sigmoid output) trained with Adam and early stopping. Achieved 84.59% accuracy (75-25 split) and 79.45% (10-fold CV), outperforming all individual models. SHAP identified CatBoost Raw, monocyte %, and RDW-CV as dominant biomarkers; LIME confirmed Total Platelet Count and RDW-CV as key negative-class drivers. ROC-AUC: 0.9616. Published: IEEE ICCIT 2025.
