Research Projects

🧠
Brain Tumor Classification — MedGemma + LoRA vs Federated Learning
Fine-tuned MedGemma-4b-it using LoRA (rank 16, 4-bit quantization) on a 30,000-sample augmented MRI corpus across four classes (glioma, meningioma, pituitary, no-tumor). Achieved 99.25% accuracy with a 0.9925 weighted F1-score, surpassing both FL-VGG16 (99.08%) and SGD-VGG16 (99.00%). Benchmarked against CNN+SVM, Ensemble, and U-Net baselines.
MedGemma-4BLoRA / QLoRAAlbumentationsFederated LearningHuggingFace
⚡
DESCO Smart Grid Demand Forecasting
Engineered a 39-feature hourly electricity demand dataset from DESCO consumption logs, integrating cyclic calendar encodings, lag features, and Open-Meteo weather API. Benchmarked 13+ models (ML, statistical, deep learning) with Optuna hyperparameter search. Proposed Optuna-tuned XGBoost (Exp D) as the optimal model (R²=0.9998, MAE=2.19 MW, MAPE=0.25%). Two-tier web app: near-term via XGBoost + Open-Meteo, long-range via fine-tuned Chronos-2. Deployed via FastAPI on Render, React on Vercel.
XGBoostOptunaChronos-2FastAPIReactSHAP
🩺
Breast Cancer Classification — XAI Soft-Voting Ensemble
Compared nine supervised ML algorithms on the Wisconsin Breast Cancer (Diagnostic) dataset. Built a soft-voting ensemble of Logistic Regression, LightGBM, and Random Forest — achieving 98.25% accuracy, 100% precision, and ROC-AUC 0.9970. Engineered a multi-layered XAI pipeline (SHAP, LIME, PFI, PDP, ALE) identifying texture worst, area worst, and concave points worst as primary malignancy indicators.
LightGBMRandom ForestSHAPLIMEPDPALE
🦟
Dengue Classification — Stacked Ensemble with XAI
Developed a stacked MLP ensemble on 1,523 hematological samples (19 features: platelet count, WBC, RDW-CV, etc.) using CatBoost and TabNet as base learners. Evaluated via stratified 10-fold CV (79.45%) and 75%-25% split (84.59%). Applied SHAP and LIME to identify CatBoost Raw, monocyte percentage, and RDW-CV as key biomarkers. Published at IEEE ICCIT 2025.
CatBoostTabNetStacked MLPSHAPLIME
📚
Multimodal LLM Systematic Review — Tripartite Safety Blueprint
Systematic review of 204 peer-reviewed articles (filtered from 8,650 across PubMed, Scopus, arXiv, IEEE Xplore) on multimodal clinical LLMs. Taxonomized foundational models (MedGemma, LLaVA-Med, SkinVL, Med-Flamingo) and 30+ clinical datasets. Proposed the Tripartite XAI-CD-FAREBIO diagnostic safety blueprint addressing hallucination, causal discovery, and fact-grounded benchmarking. Target: IEEE Access.

💳
AI Credit Risk for SME Lending — Bangladesh Bank
Integrated BERT text embeddings with XGBoost structured features for SME loan risk assessment. Conducted the first survey of unstructured data gaps across major Bangladeshi banks. Achieved AUC 0.881 and 79.2% accuracy vs 63.2% structured-only baseline. First-author publication at IEEE IDAA 2025 — proceedings in IEEE Xplore.
